    How To Play

    🎲
    Rolling & Moving: Roll the 3D dice by clicking the button or pressing Spacebar. Your pawn advances tile by tile.
    🪜
    Ladders: Landing on the base of a golden ladder instantly shoots your pawn up to the higher destination!
    🐍
    Snakes: Stepping on a serpent's head bites and slides your pawn down to its tail!
    ⚡
    Power-Up Tiles:
    • 🛡️ Snake Shield (44): Absorbs and immunizes against your next snake bite!
    • ⚡ Speed Boost (15): Instantly propels you +3 tiles forward!
    • 🌀 Cosmic Warp (82): Warps you +4 tiles forward!
    🎯
    Lucky Six & Victory: Rolling a 6 grants an immediate extra roll! Reach Tile 100 to be crowned Champion. Over-rolling bounces back from 100!
    👑

    VICTORY!

    🚀 Player 1

    Has conquered the Cosmic Serpents and reached Tile 100!
